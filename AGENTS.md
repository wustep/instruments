# Working in this repo (agents and people)

This codebase is yours: what it's for and how it's built are your call, and you'll keep building on it.

`instruments` is a browser app of fifteen acoustic instruments, each computed as physics in an AudioWorklet (TypeScript), never as samples. The instruments on `main` are the piano, the violin, the viola, the cello, the double bass, the harp, the flute, the oboe, the clarinet, the bassoon, the horn, the trumpet, the trombone, the tuba and the timpani. Production is https://instruments-wustep.vercel.app (`wustep/instruments`). `main` deploys there automatically.

## Architecture

A pnpm workspace: `packages/*` and `apps/*`.

* **`packages/engine`** — the model. It has no Web Audio dependency in the core, so the same code runs in the worklet and in Node (tests, calibration, offline renders).
  * `src/core` — the DSP: `instrument.ts` (ids, families, ranges), `factory.ts`, `pump.ts` (cores render their own blocks), `mono.ts` (one player's note handling, shared by the eight winds and brass), then one module per family (`piano-core.ts`, `bowed.ts`, `harp.ts` (47 strings as their partials, seven pedals), `flute.ts`, `oboe.ts`, `clarinet.ts`, `bassoon.ts`, `brass.ts`, `lipcone.ts` (the lips and conical bore the horn and the tuba share: `horn.ts` and `tuba.ts` are each its compass, valves and fitted voicing), `valves.ts` (the valve search of the trumpet, the horn and the tuba), `trombone.ts`, `timpani.ts`) with its fitted voicing beside it (`*-tuning.json`, `bowed-fit.json`, `calibration-fit.json`). Shared: filters, soundboard, design, precompute, render, voice, post.
  * `src/worklet` — the AudioWorklet host (`processor.ts`).
  * `src/api` — the main-thread API (`createPiano`, `createInstrument`, `renderOffline`).
  * `src/analysis` — helpers for measurements and judges.
* **`apps/web`** — the Vite page (`@piano-model/web`).
  * `main.ts`, `nav.ts`, `address.ts` — the shell, history, and the real paths (`/piano`, `/piano/upright`, `/violin`, `/viola`, `/cello`, `/bass`, `/harp`, `/flute`, `/oboe`, `/clarinet`, `/bassoon`, `/horn`, `/trumpet`, `/trombone`, `/tuba`, `/timpani`, `/orchestra`, `/measurements`, `/notes`, plus aliases `/grand`, `/upright`, `/double-bass`, `/contrabass`, `/french-horn`, `/kettledrums`, `/ensemble`). `address.ts` also writes the Orchestra's compact versioned query (`?v=1&seats=…&lines=…`), and a piece's own address: `/symphony` is "Toward Morning" on its thirteen seats (`PIECE_PATHS`, from `pieces.ts` `path`), which `nav.ts` loads on them, ready and silent.
  * `audio.ts` — talks to the worklet. `midi-file.ts`, `expression.ts`, `clock.ts`, `look.ts` (2D / vector / 3D).
  * `panel.css`, `ui/readout.ts` — the top panel: the bar (picker by family, orchestra, piano kind, view switch, pages) and the band under it (the readout of everything sounding, the spectrum, the scope or an ensemble's parts).
  * `ui/` — the 2D drawers (`stage-draw.ts`, `piano-draw.ts`, `oboe-draw.ts`, `clarinet-draw.ts`, `bassoon-draw.ts`, `horn-draw.ts`, `timpani-draw.ts`, `ensemble-draw.ts`, …), the keyboard, the player, the action close-up, tooltips.
  * `ui/vector/` — the vector (Chimera) 2D style, a second drawing of the same instruments.
  * `ui/gl/` — the 3D stage (`stage3d.ts`, `scenes3d.ts`, `gl3d.ts`, `fit3d.ts`, `fholes.ts`, and a module per instrument: `piano3d.ts`, `strings3d.ts`, `bass3d.ts`, `winds3d.ts`, `oboe3d.ts`, `clarinet3d.ts`, `bassoon3d.ts`, `horn3d.ts`, …).
  * `orchestra/` — seats, assignment, line-matching rules, the set list (`pieces.ts`), mix, the URL setup. Seating (`seats.ts`): up to four players sit on one arc in the lineup's order; five or more as an orchestra (strings in front, first violins at the audience's left, basses behind the cellos; woodwinds behind in two rows; brass at the back, the timpani past its left end or on a row behind on a phone; a harp at the left end of the first row behind the strings, by the first violins), each row behind the strings a riser higher. The 3D stage, the 2D rows (`ui/ensemble-draw.ts`) and the sound (the room's `seat`) all take it from there. The mix (`mix.ts`): one bus at 1/√Σwᵢ², each part counted at the level it is played over its calibration (the engine's `PLAYBACK_DB`: the trombone's and the timpani's recording sessions' gain), and each part's own limiter told its share of the bus (`Limiter.bus`), so it works on the level heard. Auto-routing matches a line by its name, then its General MIDI program, then its place or range (`assign.ts`, `rules.ts`). A part named for its key ("Horn in F", "Corni in Es", "Clarinetto in A", "Tromba in D"; a bare "Horn" is in F) and typed at written pitch is shifted to concert pitch; a part already sounding stays. Lines are judged by their core range, so a few pedal notes do not send a horn to the double bass.
  * `views/` — the stage, the Measurements page, the Notes page.
  * `404.html`, `src/not-found.css` — a second page of the build (`vite.config.ts` `input`): what the host serves, with status 404, for an address that is none of the app's. Its own small sheet, the app's type and colours, no app. `public/favicon.svg` is the icons' one drawing; `scripts/icons.mjs` makes `favicon.ico` and `apple-touch-icon.png` from it.
* **`e2e/`** — Playwright: Chrome at a desktop (1440×900) and a Pixel 7, and a WebKit smoke test (`e2e/webkit.spec.ts` alone, in a Mac's window and an iPhone 14). Shared cases live in `app.spec.ts`; an instrument usually adds `e2e/<id>.spec.ts`, `<id>-2d.spec.ts` and `<id>-3d.spec.ts`.
* **`scripts/`** — calibration, judges, fits, renders, screenshots, and `check-worklet-syntax.mjs`.
* **`docs/`** — research, calibration, performance, 3D reference.
* **`vercel.json`** — a static build (`pnpm build` → `apps/web/dist`) and the SPA rewrites that make every path above serve `index.html`. Any other path is the host's 404, served with the build's `404.html`: no rewrite may catch every path.

## The gate, before a merge

There are no GitHub Actions on this repo. The gate is local, then Vercel:

1. `pnpm typecheck`
2. `pnpm build` (the Safari 14.1 worklet check is part of this)
3. `pnpm test` (Vitest). This includes `apps/web/test/vercel-paths.test.ts`: every path `INSTRUMENT_PATHS` / `PIECE_PATHS` / `formatAddress` can write (`/symphony` among them), and `/orchestra`, `/ensemble`, `/measurements`, `/notes`, must be rewritten to `/index.html` in **both** `vercel.json` rewrite groups. A missing rewrite is a 404 on a reload or a shared link in production; Vite's own server answers every path, so nothing local sees it unless this test fails.
4. Playwright: `pnpm e2e`, or `E2E_PORT=<port> npx playwright test` as above. Desktop and Pixel 7 in Chrome; the projects `webkit` and `webkit-phone` run `e2e/webkit.spec.ts` alone (Safari's engine: the worklet's sound, the 3D stage, the Orchestra; `npx playwright install webkit` once; its sound is muted in the test). Two known flakes — rerun them, do not loosen them: desktop “bow in the hand”; the phone or desktop action close-up `.av-speed` is sometimes empty.
5. `main` deploys to Vercel automatically. Do not log in to the Vercel CLI. Do not rename the repo, the Vercel project or the domain. A branch named `claude/*`, `codex/*` or `agent/*` builds no preview (`vercel.json` `ignoreCommand`). After a merge, confirm the production paths return HTTP 200 with no `x-vercel-error: NOT_FOUND`.

Push small commits, each with its numbers in the message. Squash-merge only when the gate is green.

## A new instrument

One pull request, the same shape each time:

* A core in `packages/engine/src/core`, registered on `INSTRUMENT_IDS` / `INSTRUMENT_INFO` / the factory. Fit it to the University of Iowa MIS (or another licensable source) with a held-out judge of BETTER (`npx tsx scripts/judge.ts --instrument <id> --before <old engine>`); keep the change only with that verdict. Cite the physical sources in comments.
* 2D in **both** styles: the default filled drawing and the vector style.
* 3D, including how a seated player holds it in the Orchestra, and the phone frame (`gl/fit3d.ts`) so the instrument is whole and clear of the player.
* An Orchestra voice and routing: its names and aliases, its General MIDI program, in `orchestra/assign.ts` and `orchestra/rules.ts`. Lines are matched by name, then program, then place or range. Transposing parts named for a key and typed at written pitch are shifted to concert; parts already sounding stay.
* Paths: add the id and any aliases so `INSTRUMENT_PATHS` in `address.ts` stays in lockstep with both rewrite groups in `vercel.json`. `apps/web/test/vercel-paths.test.ts` must stay green. (The oboe shipped in #30 without `/oboe` in `vercel.json`; #31 added the test so that cannot happen again.)
* Unit tests for the core, the drawers, the 3D module and the routing, plus e2e alone (both 2D styles and 3D) and among the others, including the Orchestra.
* The picker: add the id to its family (`groupOf` in `views/stage.ts`). On a computer the fifteen sit on one row of the bar with their whole names clear of each other (wider than 1320 px; narrower, and on a phone, one button opens them as a sheet, four to a row, each at least 44 px to touch, every row a kind: what is struck or plucked (the keys' family, the harp in it, then the percussion's beside it), the strings, the woodwinds, the brass; the piano's kind is not in the grid: it is with the view in the bar, or on a phone at the sheet's foot). `e2e/top-panel.spec.ts` measures both; a sixteenth must keep them green.
* Notes and Measurements as the instrument needs them; before/after pictures and audio, and a `NOTES.md`, with the PR.
* The stage stays nearly wordless: no new permanent explanatory text, no position-line tooltip. The action close-up / per-string zoom is piano-only.

## MIDI and recordings

* Pieces bundled under `apps/web/public/pieces` must be public domain (Mutopia and the like), with their source, credit and sha256 in `orchestra/pieces.ts`. Never commit a copyrighted MIDI. Local-only files stay local (they are not in the repo).
* One bundled piece is the project's own: `fable-toward-morning.mid`, "Toward Morning", a symphonic movement written for these instruments by Fable 5.2 and given to the public domain (CC0 1.0). Stephen allowed it for this piece alone: any other original stays local unless he says so, and nothing copyrighted (the Daylight quintet) is ever bundled. Its address is `/symphony`; the thirteen seats' play icon plays it (`pieceForLineup`), the symphony's eighteen still play Beethoven.
* University of Iowa MIS reference recordings are for analysis only: never played as the instrument, never committed. They live in `.cache/` (typically `.cache/iowa` and `.cache/iowa-ens`). `git ls-files | grep -iE '\.(aiff?|wav|flac)$'` prints nothing.

## Deploying

`main` deploys to Vercel automatically. Push small commits, each with its numbers in the message.

Don't log in to the Vercel CLI. Don't rename the repo, the Vercel project or the domain. A branch named
`claude/*`, `codex/*` or `agent/*` builds no preview (`vercel.json` `ignoreCommand`).

## Notes for the DSP

* Units are SI and sample rates are real: test at 44.1 and 48 kHz, plus 22.05 and 96 kHz in e2e.
* Physical parameters come from the literature or are fitted to recordings, and the source is cited in a comment.
* Never allocate in the audio path. The visual telemetry (`getVisual`) runs at about 30 Hz and must stay cheap.
