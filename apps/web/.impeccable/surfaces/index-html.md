# Surface brief: the Instruments app (stage, Orchestra, player, Notes, Measurements, 404)

Scope: the whole web app at `apps/web`. Visitor mode: Experience on the stage, with Operate's discipline for
every control and Read on the two text pages. Build path: code-led (no image generation in this harness).

Audience and job: someone on a shared link, on a computer or a phone, who wants to hear and see an instrument
work; one gesture to the first note. Content and proof: the sixteen instruments themselves, live. Constraints:
every rule in AGENTS.md (heights, the picker's row and sheet, the player's box, the stage nearly wordless), no
engine change, no theme picker, the banned faces.

Unresolved: the Orchestra's four rows of the same glyphs are quieted here, not merged (behaviour, outside this
brief).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The instrument is on a stage, in a hall, under one light that comes up when it first sounds; the
chrome is the hall's printed matter. It refuses the category's night-mode bench: near-black, an orange accent,
monospace labels, a studio void.

OWN-WORLD: Claret-black hall; a garnet velvet bar; lacquer and brass on the fallboard, red felt over ivory
keys; gold for whatever is lit, chosen or sounding; verdigris for reference; ivory programme paper with sepia
ink and claret for everything that opens. Libre Caslon speaks, Cabin capitals label, italics are stage
directions. Chosen things are lit, never boxed.

STORY: A visitor sees an instrument waiting on a dim stage, touches a key, and the light comes up with the
note. They change instrument on the velvet rail, open the programme for a piece, and read the notes on paper.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The stage fills the window between a 60 px velvet bar (the sixteen instruments, the view, two
pages) and the fallboard over 88 ivory keys. The instrument stands centred in a pool of light on dark boards,
the house falling away to claret-black at the edges. The primary action is the keys themselves, full width at
the bottom; the lamp's italic "tap a key" is the one instruction.

Signature interaction: the first sound brings the spot up and the house down. Motion grammar: light fades,
nothing slides; 0.9–1.4 s for the hall's light, 120–180 ms for controls, none under reduced motion.

FORM: Evening Hall, a concert hall at night. seed: none (pinned) "A warm concert hall at night. The instrument
sits on a stage under a stage spotlight; the house beyond is dark and warm (wood, velvet, gold, a soft falloff
into the hall)." "Type and chrome should feel like a hall program or a well-printed ticket."

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
